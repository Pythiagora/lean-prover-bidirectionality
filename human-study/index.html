Lean proof readability — a short expert study

What this is. A short study for Lean-literate mathematicians. You will see ~40 short Lean snippets in which the identifiers have been renamed to placeholders (F1, F2, …) and, sometimes, comments/numbers removed. Each comes with an English sentence. Your only job: judge whether the English sentence correctly describes what the Lean snippet says or does.

Why. We are testing whether the mathematical content of a formal proof/statement is recoverable from its structure alone (without the suggestive names) — and we need to know whether humans agree with an automated measure. Your ratings are the ground truth.

Time. ~45–75 min. Anonymous. No right answers are shown. Some snippets are deliberately stripped of their bodies, and some English sentences are deliberately mismatched — so a low score is often the correct call. Trust your reading.

Notation. Math is rendered (LaTeX). A reference to another result appears as “the lemma «…»” / “the definition «…»” — treat it as a named black-box fact you need not unfold.

How to submit. When done, just click “Submit my responses” at the bottom. Your answers are sent directly — nothing to download or email.
